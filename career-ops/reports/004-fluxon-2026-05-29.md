## OVERALL RECOMMENDATION: Do Not Apply (unless consulting model is of genuine interest)

**Score: 3.0/5** — Below the 3.5 apply threshold. Revising upward from the original 2.8 due to Alberta Inc. consulting work.

**Reasons it remains below threshold:**

1. **Client base mismatch.** Fluxon's clients are AI startups (OpenAI, Anthropic), SaaS companies (Stripe), and retail (Walmart). Erika's operational depth is in energy/industrial/field operations. She'd be onboarding to unfamiliar product categories on every engagement, rather than bringing domain depth as a differentiator.

3. **Small company dynamics.** 147 employees, bootstrapped. Limited career ladder and the consulting model means constant context-switching with no long-term domain building.

**Why the score moved up from 2.8:**
Alberta Inc. was essentially the same consulting model applied to industrial/operational technology. The director-level consulting experience (evaluating AI/ML, IIoT, autonomous robotics, supply chain optimization across $14M in initiatives) directly answers the "breadth and adaptability" requirement. Erika IS a consulting PM in her background. The gap is that Fluxon's specific client sectors are AI/SaaS/eCommerce, not industrial.

**Exception case:** If Erika is genuinely interested in transitioning toward the tech industry via consulting — working across multiple clients, building breadth in AI and SaaS product contexts — Fluxon's comp and client prestige (OpenAI, Anthropic as clients) make it a real option. But Autodesk should come first.
