**Why this is a partial fit:** The platform PM discipline (strategy, roadmap, stakeholder alignment, creating structure in ambiguous environments) is a genuine match — this is exactly what Erika does. The knowledge management and workflow platform angle (consulting IP → digital experience) is directionally similar to EAM and work management platform design. The CS degree provides credible technical engagement. Active AI tool use is now demonstrable.

**Why it doesn't clear the bar:** The minimum qualification for AI-enabled application experience is not a soft preference — Autodesk is building an AI-first discovery product and needs a PM who has shipped AI features. Behavioral analytics on ticket data is not the same as owning intelligent search, contextual discovery, or recommendation systems.

**If pursuing:** The sell strategy would be CS degree + active AI tool use + behavioral analytics (discovery-adjacent work) + platform PM discipline + "create structure where none exists." This is a stretch, but the platform domain is closer to Erika's background than Babylist or Kinaxis.
