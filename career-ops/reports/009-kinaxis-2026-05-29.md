**Level detected:** Senior PM — likely $10M+ scope, reporting to Group PM or VP of Product. The 7–10 year SaaS PM requirement signals they want proven enterprise AI PM experience, not entry into the AI PM function.

**Why the AI gap is disqualifying at screening:**
Kinaxis is actively building AI into their supply chain platform — this is a product bet that requires someone who has managed ML model improvement cycles, designed AI feature rollout programs, and understands the nuances of AI product failure modes. Erika's governance design, roadmap ownership, and industrial domain knowledge are valuable but won't clear the AI product experience screen.

**Supply chain domain match re-assessed:** With the full profile, Erika's supply chain knowledge is significantly deeper than the original evaluation captured. She designed and implemented the complete procure-to-pay workflow at Keyera — PR → PO → receiving → invoicing, 3-way match logic, financial authority approval matrix, inventory management, blanket POs — the same operational processes Kinaxis's customers are trying to manage. Her users (pipeline operations, maintenance, facilities) are Kinaxis's customers' employees. For a non-AI Senior PM role at Kinaxis focused on supply chain operations (work management, field execution, inventory, purchasing), this is a strong match that would score 3.5–4.0.

**12–18 month path:** If Erika develops AI PM experience — through TAP (hardware product with embedded systems), through an AI-adjacent role at a smaller company, or through explicit AI/ML product projects — Kinaxis non-AI Senior PM roles would be worth pursuing. Watch Kinaxis's job board specifically for Senior PM roles in supply chain operations, procurement, or field execution — the domain match on those is genuine and competitive.
