I've spent fifteen years building operational infrastructure for complex organizations — but I come to operations through a design lens. I believe the best operational frameworks are the ones people actually use, designed around how work flows naturally rather than how it's organized on paper. That's a human-centered design principle applied to organizational design, and it's how I've approached every governance framework, every intake process, and every team operating model I've built.

The operational side of this role is where my experience is most direct. At TC Energy, I built the Product Management function from blank page: operating model, career ladder, governance structure, intake SLAs, and the organizational credibility that comes from a function that visibly delivers. The team grew from a loose collection of practices to a PM function that VP and General Manager-level leadership actively relied on. At AltaGas, I designed a three-tier governance structure — Strategic Leadership Council, Program Leadership Council, Technical Advisory Council — built to how each stakeholder group actually works, not how a governance model ideally would. Both of those are design problems. The output just happens to be a team or a process rather than an interface.

My design interest isn't just professional vocabulary. I've studied design heuristics formally and applied them in product decisions. The personal product I've built most recently — TAP — is specifically designed around eliminating the administrative friction of habit tracking by applying cognitive load reduction principles. I care about design as a discipline, not as a credential. I understand why design decisions get made the way they do because I make them the same way.

I'll be direct about the gap: my operational background is enterprise technology in energy and infrastructure, not creative or design team operations. I haven't managed Figma workflows or design systems governance. What I'd bring is operational rigor that most design ops candidates don't have, plus a genuine understanding of design thinking from the practitioner side. The creative operations specifics are learnable. The governance infrastructure and organizational credibility-building I'd bring in aren't.

I'm in Calgary and happy to work in person.

Erika McCann
